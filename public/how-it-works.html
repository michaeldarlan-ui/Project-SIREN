01

Connect your meeting platform.

Link Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or Google Meet. SIREN captures transcripts automatically from recorded calls using each platform's native recording — no manual export, no copy-paste.

Don't use a supported platform yet? You can still paste a transcript directly or upload a file. SIREN works with any format and a built-in recorder is coming for teams that need it.

Rep: Before I show you anything, can you help me understand what's not working with the current process?

Rep: And how long has that been the case? Is this something that's gotten worse recently?

You can add names and roles manually, or let SIREN infer them from context and speaker labels.

03

AI grades across 7 dimensions.

For each rep on the call, SIREN reads every line of the transcript and assigns a score across seven dimensions of sales excellence - from discovery quality to next step clarity.

Each score is backed by specific evidence: exact quotes, moments where the rep succeeded or missed, and a plain-English explanation of why the score is what it is.

04

Your coaching report is ready.

The full report includes a letter grade, a numerical score, a tier label (like "Peer-Level Closer"), specific strengths, and the top one or two things to coach on.

Every coaching recommendation is tied to a specific moment in the transcript. No vague advice - just "at 14:32, when the buyer raised pricing, here's what could have gone differently."

Strength - Discovery
Strong open-ended questioning in the first 8 minutes. Buyer revealed two unplanned pain points before the rep pitched anything.
Strength - Value Framing
Every feature was tied back to the buyer's stated problem. No feature dumping. The ROI anchor at 22:15 landed well.
Coach - Next Steps
Call ended with "I'll send over the proposal and follow up next week." No specific date, no commitment from the buyer. The deal momentum is at risk.

Common questions.

Do I need a call recording integration to use SIREN? +
No. You can paste any transcript directly. If you use Gong, Chorus, Otter, or Fireflies, you can export a transcript from those platforms in a few clicks. Native integrations are on the roadmap.
What if the transcript doesn't have speaker labels? +
SIREN can work with unlabeled transcripts. You can manually tag speakers before submitting, or SIREN will attempt to infer speaker roles from context. For best results, use a transcript with speaker labels.
How long does grading take? +
Most calls grade in under 60 seconds. Longer transcripts (90 minutes or more) can take 2-3 minutes. You stay on the page and the report appears when it's ready.
Can multiple reps on the same call each get their own score? +
Yes. SIREN grades each internal participant individually. If two reps and a partner are on a call, each person gets their own report. Partner scores are tracked separately from rep scores.
How are scores calculated? +
Each of the seven dimensions has a defined point value. The AI evaluates the transcript against specific criteria for each dimension and assigns a score with written justification. The total is out of 100. A letter grade and tier label are assigned based on total score.
