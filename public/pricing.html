Frequently asked.

Is there a free trial?

We don't offer a self-serve trial, but we'll grade 3-5 of your team's real calls during onboarding so you can see the output before you commit to anything.

What counts as a call?

Each transcript submission counts as one call, regardless of length or how many participants are on it. A 90-minute enterprise call counts the same as a 15-minute discovery call.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade at any time and the new plan takes effect immediately. Downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.

What if I go over my call limit on Starter?

We'll reach out before you hit the cap. You can upgrade to Growth or purchase additional calls at $4 per call for the remainder of the month.

Is my call data kept private?

Yes. Your transcripts are not used to train any AI model. Enterprise plans include data residency options and a signed DPA.

Do you offer annual pricing?

Annual billing is available on Growth and Enterprise plans, with two months free compared to monthly billing. Contact us to set that up.
